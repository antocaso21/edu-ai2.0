The project

A practical European model for responsible AI

EDU-AI 2.0 builds on the predecessor EDU-AI partnership to move from introductory training to validated, differentiated AI competence development for VET educators.

Why EDU-AI 2.0 exists

Teachers are already using AI in professional education, but their needs differ significantly by subject, teaching role, prior experience and professional sector. A single generic learning route is not enough.

The project combines an evidence-based framework, a diagnostic self-assessment and training pathways spanning foundation, intermediate and advanced competences. Data literacy, ethics, GDPR and critical evaluation are integrated throughout.

Project objectives

01

Define a VET framework

Build and validate an AI competence model for vocational teaching.

02

Diagnose learning needs

03

Personalise upskilling

Offer progressive online modules linked to individual profiles.

04

Support policy uptake

Turn cross-country evidence into transferable recommendations.

The project

Project results, accessible for everyone

The platform is designed as the central open-access home for the validated competence framework, the diagnostic instrument, the learning course and policy guidance.

Four core outputs

WP2 · D2.1

AI competence framework for VET

A transnationally validated reference framework describing competence domains across three proficiency levels. Expected length: 30–40 pages.

Planned resource · not yet available
WP2 · D2.2

AI competence diagnostic

A 30–40-item online assessment differentiated by teaching role, producing competence profiles and recommended learning pathways.

Planned tool · not yet active
WP3 · D3.2

Advanced online AI training

Nine modules across foundation, intermediate and advanced learning levels; 50–60 learning hours, with applied tasks and knowledge checks.

View course structure →
WP4 · D4.5

Policy recommendations

Evidence-based guidance for responsible AI adoption in VET, to be developed from pilot findings and international implementation experience.

Evidence-dependent · planned for 2028

Open access, multilingual and sustainable

The final dissemination platform must be available in English and partner languages. It will provide downloadable outputs, news and event materials, learner progress tracking and certificates, and remain accessible for at least five years after the project ends.

Prototype scope: English and Italian editorial pages are available here. Lithuanian, Dutch and Greek localisation, actual learning objects, user accounts, certificate engine and analytics require partner approval and technical development by ART+INN.

The project

A learning pathway shaped by your needs

Training will be linked to diagnostic results, so VET teachers can focus on their own competence gaps rather than follow one uniform course.

Three progressive levels

01

Foundation

Build essential confidence in pedagogical and responsible AI use.

02

Intermediate

Apply AI to vocational teaching tasks and sector-specific cases.

03

Advanced

Critically evaluate and integrate AI in complex VET workflows.

Six competence domains

01Pedagogical AI integration
02Occupation-specific AI applications
03Data literacy
04Ethical risk management and bias mitigation
05Data protection and GDPR compliance
06Critical evaluation of AI outputs

What the final learning environment will offer

The course will contain approximately nine modules (three per level) and correspond to 50–60 learning hours. Each module will combine micro-learning units, video explanations, sector-specific case studies, practice tasks, reflection and knowledge checks.

The production curriculum and module titles will be agreed under WP3. Learner profiles, progress monitoring and downloadable certificates are specifications for the future live system, not features of this static preview.

PROJECT BACKGROUND

From EDU-AI to EDU-AI 2.0

The predecessor KA210-VET project trained 107 educators. EDU-AI 2.0 builds on that experience with a diagnostic-based and more differentiated European approach.

Media and newsletters

Three project newsletters and at least three European-platform articles or case studies are planned. The first newsletter is currently in draft form and will be distributed only after consortium approval.

The project

Bringing research, educators and decision-makers together

National multiplier events and a European policy roundtable will support adoption of EDU-AI 2.0 results within VET systems.

Scheduled activities

01

National dissemination

Multiplier events will present validated project results to VET teachers, institutional leaders and other stakeholders in participating countries. Planned phase: May–June 2028.

02

Practical workshops

TECHIN and ERGASIA will organise additional practical workshops for teachers using the diagnostic and training tools.

03

European policy dialogue

A European online policy roundtable will connect VET practitioners and policymakers. Planned phase: June–July 2028.

Event materials and evidence

Once activities take place, this page will publish approved agendas, event materials, practical resources and summaries. Participation and evaluation evidence will be collected in line with project monitoring and data protection requirements.
